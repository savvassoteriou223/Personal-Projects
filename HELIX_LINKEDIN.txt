TITLE:  Helix - science-based training app (Android, Google Play)
DATES:  April 2026 - Present
URL:    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.helixfit.app

DESCRIPTION:

Solo-built React Native / Expo app, live on Google Play since July 2026.

Helix generates resistance-training programmes from published research rather
than from a language model. A deterministic engine turns 183 exercises across
23 movement patterns into one of 11 splits, filtered by the user's equipment,
training age, sex, goals and injuries - with per-muscle weekly set counts
solved to land inside published volume ranges.

The programme engine is verified, not asserted. Three automated harnesses run
against the real generator on every change: volume-in-range and correct
muscle-day assignment (programAudit), muscle-HEAD level coverage
(headCoverage), and every split x tier x sex combination (splitDesign):
792/792 programs clean, 24/30 head-coverage checks clean (one documented,
session-budget-constrained gap), 66/66 splits.

The most interesting piece is the Adaptive Response Engine: it fits a per-lift
adaptation-rate model from passively logged sets (Epley est-1RM -> OLS slope +
R-squared, each with a confidence score), ranks candidate experiments by value
of information, designs a pre-registered two-arm n-of-1 trial with the outcome
measure and decision rule locked before it starts, edits the programme to run
it, then judges the result statistically. It reports null results as real
findings and stays silent below 60 logged sets and 4 weeks of history, because
it cannot model the user honestly yet.

Recent work: a finish-screen share flow (a 4:5 summary card plus a 9:16
transparent "story sticker" headlining a PR or streak, only when one
genuinely happened), a paywall that now reads its trial and savings claims
from the live store offering instead of hardcoded copy, and a fix to the AI
coach reading the wrong training week against the user's own charts.

Stack: React Native 0.85 / React 19, Expo SDK 56, Supabase (Postgres + RLS, 27
migrations), 5 Deno edge functions, Anthropic Claude with tool use for the
programme-editing coach, RevenueCat, Sentry, i18next across 8 languages at
100% coverage with a build-failing drift test. ~38k LOC, 218 unit tests, 153
commits.

PART 2 - THE FULL ANALYSIS (background material, not for pasting)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT IS, IN ONE LINE

An Android fitness app that generates resistance-training programmes from
published research tables via a deterministic engine, then runs an n-of-1
experiment loop on your logged data to find what actually works for your body.

THE CORE DISTINCTION: the AI does not write the workout. Research tables do. The
AI coach is a separate layer that edits an existing programme. This is the
differentiator - most competitors claim the opposite, and blurring the two
destroys the pitch.


1. THE PROGRAMME ENGINE
   screens/programGenerator.js - 3,557 lines, deterministic, no LLM in the path.

   Training splits ....... 11 (2-6 days/week: Full Body 2x/3x/4x/5x, Upper/
                           Lower 4x and 6x, PPL 3x and 6x, a chest-back-
                           shoulders-legs 4x split, a hybrid 3x split, and a
                           PPL/Upper/Lower 5x hybrid)
   Exercises ............. 183 across 23 movement patterns
                           (screens/movementLibrary.js, 4,320 lines)
   Inputs ................ goal, training age, sex, days available, session
                           length, equipment, injuries, health conditions, sports
   Equipment tiers ....... full gym / limited (dumbbells) / bodyweight-only -
                           bodyweight users get a complete programme, back and
                           biceps included
   Sex-specific .......... women get glute-tilted accessories, men quad-tilted;
                           compound base identical, volume in range for both

   MUSCLE-HEAD RESOLUTION is the technical flex: it distinguishes upper/mid/lower
   chest, both triceps heads, both biceps heads, gastrocnemius vs soleus. Most
   apps program "chest day."

   Both the exercise pool and the split count grew materially since the last
   pass: exercises rose from 163 to 183 (new cable/machine chest, triceps,
   back, forearm and core movements; several misfiled exercises reassigned
   to the correct muscle head), and the audited split count rose from 10 to
   11 as the harness rewrite below stopped letting the ranking algorithm hide
   splits that were never actually generated.

   VERIFICATION HARNESSES (scripts/) - the part an engineering audience respects:
     programAudit.mjs   sweeps generated programmes for weekly sets per muscle
                        inside research range, correct muscle on correct day,
                        no duplicated work. Rewritten to pin every split
                        explicitly rather than letting selectSplit() choose,
                        after an earlier version audited only whichever splits
                        the ranking happened to pick and never touched the rest.
                        792/792 clean.
     headCoverage.mjs   every muscle HEAD trained, not just every muscle.
                        24/30 clean. The one gap: Full Body 2x's calf slot can
                        only run one calf exercise per week per sex, so it
                        covers the gastrocnemius head and misses the soleus
                        head - both of that split's sessions are already at
                        their 9-exercise session-time budget, so closing this
                        needs a slot rotation, not a quick fix, and it is
                        tracked rather than hidden.
     splitDesign.mjs    every split x experience tier x sex combination.
                        66/66 pass, 7 of those via a documented waived-by-design
                        list (e.g. Full Body 2x at advanced tier cannot reach
                        the rear-delt/triceps floor from a single weekly slot -
                        adding a second slot would blow the session's exercise
                        budget, so the shortfall is accepted and logged, not
                        masked as a pass).

   Set counts are solved offline and baked into a SPLIT_DAYS table; they are never
   hand-edited without re-running the harnesses.


2. THE ADAPTIVE RESPONSE ENGINE
   lib/adaptiveResponseEngine.js + lib/individualModel.js + lib/areStore.js -
   the genuine moat, live in production. Spot-checked against source for this
   revision; the mechanism below is unchanged from the last verified pass.

   Every other app applies population averages to individuals. This one runs an
   actual experiment:

     1. Individual Response Model - Epley est-1RM per set -> per-day working max
        -> OLS slope + R-squared = your adaptation rate per lift, each with an
        honest confidence score
     2. Value-of-information ranking - finds the parameter it is LEAST certain
        about that MATTERS MOST to you and is RESOLVABLE in a reasonable trial
     3. Pre-registered protocol - two arms, one variable, outcome measure and
        decision rule locked BEFORE the trial starts
     4. Programme is edited to run the trial
     5. Result judged with real statistics, verdict written permanently into
        your model

   The rigour guardrails are the product:
     - Explicit consent required; user can end a trial at any time
     - ONE TRIAL AT A TIME, enforced at the database level (a CHECK constraint
       in the migration, not just app logic) - two would contaminate each
       other and neither answer could be trusted
     - "NO DIFFERENCE" IS A REAL, REPORTED RESULT - it rules that variable out
       for you permanently
     - SILENT below 60 logged sets AND 4 weeks of history (both constants live
       in lib/areStore.js) - it will not model you dishonestly
     - Will not propose a trial whose outcome it cannot measure

   The willingness to return a null result is the credibility.


3. THE AI COACH
   Supabase Edge Function supabase/functions/ai-coach/index.ts
   -> Claude Sonnet with tool use + prompt caching.

   CAN: replace/add/remove exercises, adjust sets, reps, effort - permanently or
   next-session-only; return ranked alternatives with reasoning when you don't
   name a substitute; remember dislikes, injuries, preferences and goals across
   sessions (viewable and deletable); work mid-workout on the exercise in front
   of you; write a weekly narrative review.

   A real bug closed since the last pass: the coach was reading name-only sets
   through a path that discards any set missing both reps and weight, so its
   volume table read all-zero for every muscle while the same-screen chart it
   sat next to was full. It also lacked any date anchor, so a session from
   yesterday could read as old, and its weekly review cached a still-in-progress
   week's summary for the rest of that week. Fixed by making the coach and the
   chart pull rows through the same path, anchoring "today," and reviewing only
   the week that has actually ended.

   CONTEXT IT RECEIVES: profile, full programme, equipment, injuries, weekly
   volume vs target, PRs, recent lifting and cardio, nutrition logs, plateau and
   deload status, recovery check-ins, bodyweight trend, and everything it has
   remembered.

   Constrained to cite the 74-study library (screens/studiesLibrary.js, 71 unique
   citations) rather than improvise.

   CANNOT: change your split, adjust nutrition targets, reschedule training days,
   set your working weights, or contact you between sessions.

   Honest framing: it is a PROGRAMME EDITOR WITH YOUR TRAINING FILE OPEN, not a
   chatbot. 100 messages/month, premium only.


4. EVERYTHING ELSE THAT SHIPS
   - Workout logging - sets, reps, weight, rest timer, mid-session swaps
     (screens/WorkoutExecutionScreen.jsx, 1,861 lines)
   - Workout sharing - a Share button on the finish screen opens a 4:5 preview
     card (duration, sets, volume, best set per exercise, streak) before the OS
     share sheet; a bodyweight-only session falls back to reps instead of a
     false "0 KG LIFTED". Guarded behind try/require so an older binary
     degrades to plain-text share instead of crashing.
   - Story sticker - a 9:16 transparent PNG headline for a finished session,
     built to sit on top of a photo the user was already going to post rather
     than ask to BE the post. lib/achievements.js walks a fixed priority
     ladder over data already stored (PR, streak milestone, heaviest session,
     muscles trained) and always returns the rarest thing that's TRUE - a
     first-ever exercise is explicitly not a PR, and a session has to beat its
     predecessor by a 3% margin with at least three prior sessions logged
     before it counts as "heaviest," so the sticker can't manufacture a
     compliment nobody would believe.
   - Day trimming - a program day can now be cut to a shorter session from the
     Program screen, not just from Today; both screens share one
     plannedVolumeFloor() so a trim means the same thing in both places.
   - Exercise demonstration gifs - re-hosted into a Supabase table rather than
     hotlinked, so playback doesn't depend on a third-party catalogue staying
     up or a key surviving in the client bundle.
   - Muscle recovery map - front/back body SVG anatomy, fresh vs recovering
   - Weekly volume tracking - sets per muscle vs research target, head-level
     detail
   - Nutrition - targets that shift on training vs rest days; photo, voice, text
     and barcode logging (GPT-4o-mini + Open Food Facts); 7-day protein history
   - Body composition - weight trend, 7-day moving average, progress to target
   - Cardio logging, PRs, plateau and deload detection with the research behind
     each
   - Recovery check-ins - self-reported sleep/soreness/energy adjusts session
     effort
   - The "why" layer - tap almost any number, see the study

6. HONEST WEAKNESSES - DO NOT OVERSELL THESE
   - It is a LOGGING APP FIRST - a user who does not log gets little
   - The Adaptive Response Engine needs 60 logged sets and 4 weeks of history;
     it is a retention asset, not an install-day hook
   - The coach CANNOT INITIATE - no notifications, no check-ins
   - Coaching visuals are gifs and written cues, not video - a 3D exercise
     figure viewer exists in the code (screens/Exercise3DModal.jsx) but is not
     imported or reachable from any screen; treat as unshipped
   - Sharing is ONE-WAY OUT of the app (finish-screen share sheet to the OS
     share surface) - there is still no in-app social layer: no feed, no
     follows, no leaderboards
   - NO WEARABLE / HEALTH INTEGRATION. Health Connect was removed outright after
     a Play policy rejection; all four health permissions are blocked in the
     manifest. Recovery is self-reported.
   - Verified by harnesses and smoke tests, not a large device beta


7. CLAIMS TO NEVER MAKE
   - Anything implying AI WRITES THE WORKOUT - it does not, and that is the
     differentiator
   - HRV, heart-rate, sleep or STEP TRACKING - removed for Play policy
   - iOS AVAILABILITY
   - Any specific USER COUNT, RATING OR RESULT - there is no data to support one
