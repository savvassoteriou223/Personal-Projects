## 1. One-line description
Helix is an AI-powered fitness and nutrition app that builds you a personalized
training program, coaches you day to day, and lets you log meals just by taking a
photo or speaking.

## 2. Elevator pitch (longer)
Most fitness apps make you do the work: type in every food, guess your program,
and figure out recovery yourself. Helix flips that. It generates a science-backed
workout program tailored to your goals, experience, sex, and equipment; it lets
you log nutrition by snapping a photo of your plate or saying what you ate; and it
gives you a coach that remembers your preferences and injuries and adjusts your
plan when you ask. It even reads your sleep and heart rate to flag low-recovery
days so you train smart, not just hard.

## 3. Who it's for (target audience)
- Primary: adults 20–35 who train 3–5x per week, or want to start, and are tired
  of manual calorie logging and generic cookie-cutter programs.
- Secondary: beginners who feel lost in the gym and want structure without hiring
  a personal trainer; intermediate/advanced lifters who want research-backed
  programming and recovery guidance.
- Mindset: values evidence over bro-science, wants results without spending hours
  on admin, is comfortable with an app doing the thinking.

## 4. The core problems Helix solves
1. Logging food is tedious → snap a photo or speak, done in seconds.
2. Programs are generic or overwhelming → a personalized, science-backed plan
   built for your body and equipment.
3. People overtrain or train randomly → recovery signals + a coach that adapts.
4. Personal trainers are expensive → an AI coach that knows your history, in your
   pocket, for a subscription.

## 5. Key features (and the benefit of each)

**Photo & voice food logging**
- Take a photo of a meal → automatic calorie and macro estimates. Log food by
  voice. Barcode scanning for packaged items.
- Benefit: track nutrition in seconds instead of minutes of typing and searching.

**AI Coach**
- A conversational coach that knows your profile, goals, equipment, and training
  history. Remembers your preferences, injuries, dislikes and goals across
  sessions (and you can view/delete what it remembers). Suggests ranked,
  evidence-based exercise swaps. Writes a weekly summary of your training.
- Benefit: personal-trainer-style guidance that actually knows you, and gets
  smarter the more you use it.

**Personalized workout program generator**
- Builds training splits based on experience, goals, sex, and available equipment
  (full gym, limited equipment, or bodyweight only). Volume-scaled and
  research-backed. Adapts around injuries/health conditions. Tracks progressive
  overload and learns your typical weight jumps.
- Benefit: a plan built for you specifically, from day one, no guesswork.

**Recovery awareness**
- Reads sleep duration and resting heart rate (plus heart-rate variability and
  steps on iPhone) to compute a daily Recovery status (Ready / Moderate / Low),
  shown on the home/profile screen. On low days it flags you and recommends
  lighter loads or rest, and the coach will tailor an adjustment if you ask.
- Benefit: train hard when you're ready, back off when you're not — avoid burnout
  and injury.

**Science-backed content**
- Exercise, calisthenics, and movement libraries with technique guidance, plus a
  studies library citing the research behind recommendations.
- Benefit: trust that advice is grounded in evidence, not fads.

**Other**
- Free tier plus Helix Pro subscription. Multi-language support.

## 6. What makes Helix different (differentiators)
- Photo/voice nutrition logging removes the #1 reason people quit tracking.
- The coach has persistent memory of you — it's not a generic chatbot; it learns
  your body, preferences and history.
- Recovery-aware: connects sleep/heart-rate data to training decisions.
- Research-grounded: recommendations cite studies, not gym myths.
- All-in-one: program + nutrition + coaching + recovery in one app.

## 7. Competitors (for positioning/contrast)
- MyFitnessPal (nutrition) → Helix is far faster to log (photo vs. searching).
- Fitbod / Freeletics (workouts) → Helix adds a memory-based AI coach + nutrition
  + recovery.
- A human personal trainer → Helix is a fraction of the cost, available 24/7.
Angle: "the personalization of a coach, the convenience of an app."

## 8. Brand voice & tone
- Confident, clear, a little bold. Speaks like a knowledgeable friend who lifts,
  not a corporate wellness brand.
- Evidence-first but not preachy. No hype, no fake urgency, no shame-based
  messaging.
- Punchy and modern — fits short-form video (TikTok/Reels).

## 14. Honesty guardrails (IMPORTANT — do not overclaim)
- The app RECOMMENDS training adjustments on low-recovery days and the coach can
  edit the plan if the user accepts — it does NOT silently auto-rewrite workouts
  on its own. Phrase as "notices and recommends / your coach adjusts," not
  "automatically changes your workout without you."
- Photo nutrition gives ESTIMATES, not lab-perfect numbers. Say "estimates."
- Don't make medical or guaranteed-results claims (no "cure", "guaranteed X kg").
- iOS: only advertise it as available once it actually is.
