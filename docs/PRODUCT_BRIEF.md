# Helix — what the product actually is

A complete, honest description of the app as it exists in build 43, written so a
marketing plan can be built on it without anyone having to read the code.

Everything here is verified against the source. Where something is planned but
not shipped, it says so. Nothing in this document is aspirational.

---

## 1. The one-sentence version

**Helix builds your training programme from published resistance-training
research, then adapts it to you — and it can prove both claims.**

Two words carry the product, and they are not interchangeable:

- **Science-based** — the programme comes from research, not from a language
  model. Set counts, rep ranges, rest periods and exercise choice are derived
  from published findings and checked by automated harnesses before shipping.
- **Tailored** — the same research produces a different programme for a
  28-year-old woman with dumbbells at home than for a man with four years of
  training and a full gym.

### The distinction that matters most

**The AI does not write your workout.** This is the single most important thing
to get right in any marketing copy.

The programme is generated by a deterministic engine from research tables. The AI
coach is a separate thing that sits on top: it reads your training history and can
*edit* the programme you already have. If a competitor says "AI-generated
workouts," that is the opposite of Helix's claim, and blurring the two destroys
the differentiator.

## 2. What a user gets, in plain terms

### The programme

- **10 training splits**, from 2 to 6 days a week, chosen for you by days
  available, goal and experience
- **163 exercises across 22 movement patterns**, filtered to the equipment you
  actually have and to any injuries on file
- Every programme is checked so that **each muscle's weekly sets land inside its
  research target** — for your training age, and for your sex
- Every muscle **head** is covered: upper, mid and lower chest; both triceps
  heads; both biceps heads; gastrocnemius and soleus. Not just "chest day."
- Sessions rotate exercises between training blocks, so you are not doing the
  identical session for months

### Proof this is real, not a claim

Three automated harnesses run against the actual generator:

| Harness | What it proves | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `programAudit.mjs` | 288 real programmes — volume in range, right muscle on the right day, no duplicate work | **288 / 288 clean** |
| `headCoverage.mjs` | every muscle head is trained, not just every muscle | **30 / 30** |
| `splitDesign.mjs` | all 10 splits × 3 experience tiers × 2 sexes | **60 / 60** |

Before this work the same audit passed **18 of 144**. That number is a fair
before/after if you want one.

### The rest of the app

- **Workout logging** — sets, reps, weight, rest timer, mid-session exercise swaps
- **Muscle recovery map** — front and back body, showing what is fresh and what is
  still recovering
- **Weekly volume tracking** — sets per muscle against your research target, with
  head-level detail
- **Nutrition** — calorie and macro targets that change on training vs rest days,
  meal logging, 7-day protein history
- **Body composition** — weight trend, 7-day moving average, progress to target
- **Cardio logging** alongside lifting
- **Recovery check-ins** — daily readiness that adjusts the session's effort
- **Plateau and deload detection** — flagged automatically, with the research
  behind it
- **Personal records**
- **The "why" layer** — tap almost any number and see the study behind it, from a
  **74-study curated library**

## 3. The AI coach — exactly what it can and cannot do

### It can

- **Change your programme.** Replace an exercise, adjust sets, reps or effort,
  add an exercise, remove one. Permanently, or for the next session only.
- **Rank replacements.** Ask to change an exercise without naming a substitute
  and it returns three ranked options with the reasoning for each.
- **Remember you.** Dislikes, injuries, preferences and goals persist across
  conversations. A disliked exercise stops being programmed. You can see
  everything it remembers and delete any of it.
- **Work mid-workout.** It reads the session you are in and can swap the exercise
  you are standing in front of.
- **Write a weekly review** — a narrative summary of what you trained and what to
  focus on next.

### What it knows when you ask

Your profile, your full programme, your equipment, your injuries, weekly volume
against target, personal records, recent lifting sessions, recent cardio,
nutrition logs, plateau status, deload status, recovery check-ins, bodyweight
trend, and everything it has remembered about you.

It is also required to cite from the 74-study library rather than improvise.

### It cannot

- Change your split (you do that yourself)
- Adjust nutrition targets
- Reschedule which day you train
- Set your working weights
- Contact you between sessions — there are no coach notifications

### The honest positioning

It is a **programme editor with your training file open**, not a fitness chatbot.
The reason to use it over a free general-purpose AI is that it can change your
actual programme, it remembers what it changed, and it is constrained to cite
research rather than invent it.

**Limit: 100 AI messages per month.** Premium only.

## 4. The Adaptive Response Engine — the genuine differentiator

This is the part no competitor has, and it is live in build 43.

### What it does

Every fitness app applies population averages to individuals: *"three sessions at
the top of the rep range, add weight."* Helix runs an experiment on you instead.

1. It builds a quantified model of how **your** body responds, from sets you have
   already logged
2. It finds the thing it is **least certain** about for you
3. It designs a **controlled experiment** to answer it — two arms, one variable,
   everything else held constant, the outcome measure and decision rule fixed
   **before the trial starts**
4. It changes your programme to run the trial
5. It judges the result with real statistics and writes the answer into your
   profile — permanently

### Why the rigour is the product

- **Nothing starts without your explicit consent**, and you can end a trial at any
  time
- **One trial at a time**, enforced at the database level — two would contaminate
  each other and neither answer could be trusted
- **"No difference" is a real result.** It rules that variable out for you
  permanently. The app is built to report it rather than manufacture a win.
- **Below 60 logged sets or 4 weeks of history it says nothing at all**, because
  it cannot model you honestly yet
- It will not propose a trial whose outcome it cannot measure

### How to talk about it

The claim is not "AI personalisation" — everyone says that. The claim is:

> **Helix runs controlled experiments on your training and tells you what actually
> works for your body — including when the answer is "that made no difference."**

The willingness to return a null result is the credibility. Lead with it.

**Caveat for planning:** it needs about a month of logged training before it
activates. It is a retention and word-of-mouth asset, not an install-day hook.

## 5. Who it is for

Verified from the onboarding flow, which collects goals, equipment, experience,
training days, session length, sports, injuries and body stats.

**Core fit**
- Trains with weights and wants to know the programme is not arbitrary
- Beginner through advanced — the programme genuinely differs by training age
- Any equipment: full gym, home dumbbells, or **bodyweight only** (a bodyweight
  user gets a complete programme, back and biceps included)
- Both sexes — women get glute-focused accessory work, men get quad-focused, with
  the compound base identical and volume in range for both

**Poor fit**
- Wants a class or a follow-along video
- Wants a coach that writes a bespoke plan by chat
- Runners and endurance athletes as a primary audience — cardio is supported but
  lifting is the product

## 6. Free vs paid

**Free**
- The full generated programme
- Workout logging, rest timer, exercise swaps
- Recovery map and weekly volume
- Nutrition targets and manual meal logging
- Body weight tracking, PRs, plateau and deload alerts
- The research "why" layer

**Premium**
- **AI Coach** — 100 messages/month, programme edits, memory, weekly review
- **Smart nutrition logging** — describe a meal in words, photograph it, or log by
  voice; macros calculated for you
- **Your Data** — full history rather than a 7-day window: every logged set,
  complete nutrition log, recovery trends, cardio history

Sold as monthly or annual. **Pull exact prices from Play Console — they are set
there, not in the app, and this document will not guess.**

## 7. Platform and reach

- **Live on Google Play**, package `com.helixfit.app`, since 11 July 2026
- **iOS is configured but not shipped.** Plan Android-only for the 90 days.
- **8 languages** — English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese,
  Russian, Chinese. English is the most complete; the others fall back to English
  for the newest strings.
- **Health Connect** on Android reads **sleep and resting heart rate only**. HRV
  and step count were deliberately removed after a Play review rejection — do not
  advertise them.
- **No wearable required.** Everything the engine does works from logged sets.

## 8. Honest weaknesses

Marketing that oversells these will produce refunds and one-star reviews.

- **It is a logging app first.** The programme adapts to what you log; a user who
  does not log gets little.
- **The Adaptive Response Engine needs ~4 weeks of history** before it says
  anything.
- **The coach cannot start conversations.** No notifications, no check-ins. It
  waits to be opened.
- **No video demonstrations.** Exercises have written cues and a 3D figure;
  animated demonstrations are planned, not shipped.
- **No social, no sharing, no leaderboards.**
- **Not device-tested at scale.** The app is verified by automated harnesses and
  a signed-in smoke test, not by a large beta.

## 9. The claims you can defend

Every line here is backed by something in the repository.

- "Your programme is checked against published volume research — 288 out of 288
  generated programmes land in range."
- "Every head of every muscle gets trained. Most apps do not distinguish the long
  head of your triceps from the lateral."
- "74 studies behind the numbers. Tap any of them."
- "The workout is not written by AI. It comes from research tables, and we can
  show you the table."
- "It runs controlled experiments on your training — and tells you when something
  made no difference to you."
- "Different programme for men and women, without cutting the compound lifts from
  either."
- "Works with a full gym, a pair of dumbbells, or nothing at all."

### Claims to avoid

- Anything implying **AI writes the workout** — it does not, and this is the
  differentiator
- **HRV or step tracking** — removed for Play policy
- **iOS availability**
- Any specific **user count, rating or result** — there is no data to support one

## 10. Suggested spine for a 90-day plan

Not a plan — the raw material for one, in priority order.

**Days 1–30 — establish the category difference.** The market believes fitness AI
writes workouts. Helix's claim is the opposite. Everything early should teach that
one distinction: *research writes the programme, AI edits it for you.* The
verifiable numbers (288/288, 74 studies, 163 exercises) belong here.

**Days 31–60 — demonstrate depth.** Muscle-head coverage, the sex-specific
programming, the bodyweight-user story, the "why" layer. This is the material
that converts someone who has tried three other apps.

**Days 61–90 — the moat.** The Adaptive Response Engine, once early users have
enough history to have seen it fire. This is the content no competitor can copy,
and it lands hardest as a real user result — including a null one.
