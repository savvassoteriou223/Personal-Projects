# Helix — Volume Blueprint (study sheet)

## 1. How volume is set
- **Per exercise sets** come from the GOAL (Build muscle = 4 compound / 3 isolation), then scaled by EXPERIENCE.
- **Experience factor:** beginner ×0.8 · intermediate ×1.0 (design baseline) · advanced ×1.2. Same for men and women.
- Evidence: dose-response rises with diminishing returns and is steeper in trained lifters (Schoenfeld 2017; Pelland/Nuckols 2026 Sports Med; Enes 2024). No per-set sex difference (Refalo 2025); women tolerate the top of the band better (Hunter 2014).
- **Counting:** sets attributed to the slot primary mover. squat/leg-ext/leg-press = Quads; RDL/deadlift = Hamstrings (full); hip thrust/abduction/lunge = Glutes. Optional work excluded.
- **Two known counting effects to keep in mind while reading the matrix:**
  1. **Glutes for men look low/0** — men get glutes from squats + RDLs, shown as "(+N via compounds)". Not a real hole.
  2. **Hamstrings look high** — RDL/deadlift are counted 100% to hamstrings. Credit half to glutes/back (fairer) and the ham numbers drop ~3-4 sets.

Bands shown are the per-level OPTIMAL range from VOLUME_TARGETS. ⬇ = below band, ⬆ = above band (for the higher of M/F).

## 3. Proposed targeted tune (optional — you decide)
The scaling is the big fix and should stay. These are small, OPTIONAL per-split adjustments for the two patterns the matrix exposes. None change the structure or the sex tilt.

### A. Side delts low (band starts at 10 int / 14 adv)
Splits where side delts sit under at intermediate+: full_body_2x (6), full_body_3x (9), hybrid_3x (6), upper_lower_4x (8), bro (7), ul_ppl (9), full_body_5x (9).
- **Option:** add ONE cable-lateral slot (3 sets) on an upper/full-body day that is light on side delts. Lifts each ~+3 (e.g. hybrid_3x 6→9, upper_lower_4x 8→11). Never added to a leg/lower day (keeps the no-upper-on-lower rule).
- **Cost:** +1 exercise on those days. 2-day splits stay under regardless (inherent).

### B. Hamstrings high on posterior-heavy splits
Over-band at intermediate (before half-crediting hinges): upper_lower_4x 14, full_body_4x 15, ul_ppl 16, full_body_5x 17(M), ppl_6x 13, upper_lower_6x 15.
- Most of this is the hinge-counting effect — credit RDL/deadlift half to glutes and these mostly fall in band. The one genuinely self-inflicted case is **full_body_5x male**: the male accessory tilts on Day B and Day C both route to leg curls, stacking 5 curl slots.
- **Option 1 (full_body_5x only):** reroute ONE male tilt (Day C) off leg curl. The only lower muscle with room is calves (int 11, band 6-12) — reroute to a calf raise. Male hams 17→~14, calves 11→13. Women untouched.
- **Option 2:** leave all hamstring numbers — with fair hinge-counting they are at the top of band, not over, and posterior overdevelopment is low-risk.

### C. Pull volume on ppl_6x (back 21 int / 27 adv)
ppl_6x runs two full pull days; back sits at/over the top at advanced. Optional: drop one back-isolation set on Pull B. Low priority — back tolerates high volume well.

_Recommendation: do A (side delts, clear win) + B Option 1 (the one real ham overage). Leave C. But this is your call after studying the matrix above._
